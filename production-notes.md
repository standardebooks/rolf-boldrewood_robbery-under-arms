This edition is based on the 1889 abridged version of the novel but restores textual material cut from that edition, by transcribing the missing passages from the original serialization in the Sydney Mail newspaper. In trying to identify here the missing text now included, note that the chapter numbering becomes confusing in the serialization because the newspaper skipped chapter numbers 36 and 58. I will stick to the numbers given in the newspaper, though they don’t perfectly match those in our edition because of this. In summary:

(1) missing paragraph at the end of Chapter 17 of the serial version, starting “As for Starlight”;

(2) missing paragraph at the end of Chapter 21, starting “She turned away without another word”;

(3) missing start of Chapter 23 running to before “Because it’s too late”;

(4) missing text in the middle of Chapter 24, from “As it turned out we all got safe” through all of Chapter 25 and 26 (which were omitted in the abridged version) to before “Our next chance” in Chapter 27 of the serial version);

(5) missing end of Chapter 35 from “There was no cheering after this” through to before “We were desperately fidgety” in Chapter 37;

(6) missing end of Chapter 38 and start of Chapter 39 from “I never could make out” down to before “While we were taking it easy”;

(7) all of Chapter 45 starting “We mounted, and cleared then” down to before “We hadn’t been long at home” in Chapter 46;

(8) missing paragraphs in the middle of Chapter 55 from “Up the mountain side track” down to before “Next day after that I was at Willaroon;

(9) missing paragraph in chapter 57 starting “But didn’t I feel” down to before “Sometimes I felt”;

(10) missing end of Chapter 57 from “After a bit I got better” down to the end of the chapter.
